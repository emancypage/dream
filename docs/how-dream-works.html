One night of Dream

During the day you talk to coding agents. At 04:00 your computer goes to sleep the way a brain does. It replays the day, keeps what matters, invents practice scenes, and forgets on purpose. The next morning, new sessions remember.

The whole night on one page

Only two times are real schedules: 04:00 from dream.timer and Sundays at 12:00 from dream-audit.timer, which you enable yourself; each adds up to 15 minutes of random delay. The stage times after 04:00 are drawn for reading, not measured. Embedding runs inside the ingest stage, not as a stage of its own. The stages run one after another in dream.service. If one stage fails, the next ones still run, and the run is marked as failed health.
